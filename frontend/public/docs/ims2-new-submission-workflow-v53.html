What changed from v52

A new email-triage lane (band A) sits upstream of intake. It reads the mailbox, classifies, extracts with evidence, verifies the NPI, builds a versioned context, runs the underwriting skills, rates, and proposes the policy setup. It ends in a draft that a person accepts, edits, questions or rejects. Manual intake stays. The IMS 2 record (boxes 17 and 19) is the same record as today, pre-filled, with the rating and setup shown as an indication. Bind, post, bordereaux, endorsement, close and reinstate are unchanged. Renewal is still not built.

Two rules the lane obeys

Triage never writes to IMS 2. It produces a draft outside the intake DB; only the reviewer's accept creates the editable record, and nothing from triage ever reaches a journal directly. A proposal is not a value. The rating is an indication and the setup is a proposal until the underwriter confirms them in policy info; the confirmed values are what satisfy the bind gate and post to the policy journal.

1MailboxNEW · SOURCE

The shared submissions mailbox is watched (push, polling fallback). Every new message and attachment is read; nothing is sent, deleted or moved by the system.

2ClassifyNEW

Rules first, then the model: SUBMISSION, NOT_SUBMISSION or REVIEW_REQUIRED. Each outcome carries a confidence and the reason. Only SUBMISSION continues on its own.

3ArchiveNEW

A label is applied in Gmail and the message is logged. No IMS 2 record is created. Anyone can reopen it from the triage dashboard.

4Triage queueNEW · GATE

Low-confidence or odd messages wait here. A person confirms (continues to parse) or archives. Nothing downstream runs until they decide.

5Parse & extractNEW

Attachments are opened (OCR for scans) and the application fields are extracted: insured, specialty, state, limits, retro date, claims history, prior carrier. Every field keeps a link to the page and line it came from.

6NPI verifyNEW

The NPI registry is queried by number or name. Taxonomy, license state and practice address are compared with the application; mismatches become findings, not blockers.

7Match existing?NEW · GATE

Is this email about a record IMS 2 already has? Matched by thread, then NPI, then insured name + state. Yes: attach. No: a new intake draft.

8Attach to existingNEW

The documents and a new assessment version are attached to the existing submission (open or bound). A change request on a bound policy is routed to the Endorsement path; it never edits the bound row.

9SynthesisNEW

One consolidated, versioned context: extracted facts with sources, NPI result, conflicts between documents, missing items. This is the input to everything that follows.

10UW analysisNEW

The underwriting skills (DPL manual rules) run over the context: eligibility, risk indicators, completeness, inconsistencies, follow-up questions. Output is a versioned analysis report.

11RateNEW

A premium indication from the rating rules: specialty class, territory, limits, claims-made step, credits and debits, surcharges. It is an indication, never the bound premium.

12Policy setupNEW

The proposed policy setup: effective and expiration dates, limits, retro date, specialty class, entity or vicarious coverage, billing option. Proposed values only, each with its source or rule.

13Intake draftNEW · STAGED

Everything above, mapped to the shape of the intake DB: submission header, the POL details row, the insured master, locations and contacts, plus the rating and setup proposal and the assessment version. Held outside IMS 2 until accepted.

14Underwriter reviewNEW · GATE

A person decides. Accept creates the IMS 2 submission pre-filled. Edit corrects fields first (each edit audited against the extracted value). Follow-up drafts questions to the broker. Reject archives with a reason.

15Follow-up emailNEW

The follow-up questions go out to the broker (sent by the person, or auto-sent per D-T6). The reply lands in the same mailbox, matches the same draft by thread, and produces a new context version.

16Manual intakeUNCHANGED

Submissions that do not arrive by email are still keyed in by hand. Both paths create the same IMS 2 record.

17IMS 2 · OpenCHANGED

The editable header and its first details row. From the triage path it is created pre-filled, with each field marked as extracted, edited by the reviewer, or keyed by hand.

18AoDocsUNCHANGED

Still a manual link field. Triage stores its own copies of the attachments with the draft; filing in AoDocs stays a human step (D-T5).

19IMS 2 · Policy infoCHANGED

Policy info as today, plus the rating indication and the proposed setup shown beside the fields. The underwriter confirms or changes them; the confirmed values are what bind.

20RenewalUNCHANGED

Still not built. When it is, a renewal email will match the prior term through box 7 and open a new header chained by parent_id / chain_id.

21EndorsementUNCHANGED

POST /:id/endorsements on a bound parent. Creates a new details row (END) under the header; it then goes through policy info and bind like any transaction.

22IMS 2 · ClosedUNCHANGED

Not bound: the user closes with a reason. Reinstate returns it to Bound.

23Bound?UNCHANGED

Gate: intake, insured, broker, term and premium > 0 (END: any). The triage proposal does not satisfy the gate by itself; the underwriter's confirmed values do.

24IMS 2 · Bound 🔒UNCHANGED

Locked. DPL-800 number assigned (END inherits). Admin override returns it to Open. Bind posts the policy journal per Addendum C.

25QA batchUNCHANGED

Operators send bound rows to QA; the reviewer approves (posts) or rejects (back to draft).

26Admin · PostUNCHANGED

Admin posts a single bound record once.

27PaymentUNCHANGED

Payments, credits, refunds and adjustments post through batches into the transaction journal (Addendum C). Not touched by triage.

28Bordereaux 🔒UNCHANGED

Append-only, parent first. Per SL-L11 it is an output of the journals and is never read to create a record.

29ExportUNCHANGED

73-column export, byte-identical headers.

30BordereauxUNCHANGED

The legacy Google Sheet. A/R statement and A/R management still live there until the journals cut over.
