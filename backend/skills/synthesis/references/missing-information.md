points to). Cite the section in the `reason`, e.g. "required for new business (DPL Manual §1.3)".

## Loss runs: waiver rule

The manual lets the underwriter waive loss runs for a **Retro Date Inception** policy with no
prior acts (§1.6). You do not decide the waiver, but you set the importance:

- **RDI / no prior acts requested, and no specialty or exposure on the carve-out list below:**
  list loss runs as IMPORTANT with the reason "may be waivable for an RDI policy under DPL Manual
  §1.6; underwriter decision". List a signed **No Known Loss / No Known Incident attestation** as
  CRITICAL (it is required for the waiver).
- **Otherwise** (prior acts, a rolled retro date, any disclosed claim / incident / board matter,
  a coverage gap, an ERP request, a group / entity / medical director / vicarious exposure):
  loss runs stay CRITICAL.
- **Carve-out specialties always need loss runs (CRITICAL), even on RDI:** OB/GYN with
  deliveries, neurosurgery, spine, orthopaedic, plastic / cosmetic, interventional pain,
  cardiothoracic and vascular, bariatric, emergency medicine where DPL would be the primary
  individual policy, and anyone performing BBL or liposuction over 1,000 cc. Where loss runs are
  unobtainable the manual accepts an NPDB report plus an attestation; say so in the reason.

## Present but unusable

Also list anything present but **unusable**: unreadable scans, an unsigned or undated
application, an application that appears altered, loss runs valued more than 90 days before the
proposed effective date (or the email's received date when no effective date is stated), loss
runs covering fewer than 10 years without explanation, or redacted pages. NPI is never a missing item here;
`references/npi-verification.md` covers the one NPI case.

Give each item a specific reason of about 15 words, e.g. "Valued 2026-03-01, over 90 days before
2026-07-01 effective date (§1.3)". Name the item in `item` and don't repeat it in `reason`.
