# Document Reconciliation

1. **Inventory first.** List every item that arrived: the email itself plus each attachment,
   one row each. Give its type, its processing status, and a short note of about 12 words
   (e.g. "Signed 2026-04-02; 6 pp." or "ProAssurance, valued 2026-03-15"). Record unreadable or
   failed attachments here too, and in Missing Information if they likely held required data.

   Types: DPLRRG application, renewal application, competitor application (name the carrier),
   supplemental application (entity, midlevel, weight loss, MedSpa, locum), loss runs, NPDB
   report, declarations page, expiring policy (full copy), CV, licence / limited permit, board
   certificate, Warranty Form, No Known Claims affidavit, No Known Loss / Incident attestation,
   COI, Waiver of COI Request Form, supervision / collaborative agreement, medical director
   agreement, provider roster, consent form, training certificate, ERP request, DD&R proof
   (death certificate, disability letter), bind request, signed quote, other.

2. **Cross-check key fields** across documents: name, NPI, specialty and time split,
   procedures, state and practice locations, effective date, retroactive date, limits, prior
   carrier and policy periods, claim counts, dates and amounts, hours per week, board status,
   entity ownership.

3. **Classify each disagreement:**
   - _Formatting only_ (e.g. "J. Smith" vs "John Smith", "OB/GYN" vs "Obstetrics &
     Gynecology"): not a conflict; note the canonical form.
   - _Material_ (different claim counts, specialty, procedures, limits, retro or effective
     dates, hours, coverage periods): a conflict, handled under the SKILL.md conflict rules.

4. **Application integrity.** The manual forbids altering an application; clarifications must be
   on a separate, dated, signed sheet (DPL Manual §1.2). Record any visible alteration
   (overwritten or crossed-out answers) as a conflict, and record a clarification sheet as its own
   inventory row. Information the email says was obtained by telephone is a broker statement, so
   treat it as secondary.

5. **Coverage continuity.** Lay the prior policy periods side by side (carrier, form, dates,
   retro date). Report any gap or overlap with the dates on both sides, and any period written on
   an occurrence form, as facts. Disagreement between the application and the declarations pages
   about these periods is a conflict.
