# DPL Underwriting Manual: Facts to Capture

The facts each rule of the **DPLRRG Underwriting Manual (v5.7.26, effective 2026-05-07)** depends
on: the Analysis can only apply a rule to a fact you established. Capture every one the evidence
supports, with its source, and carry the material ones into `consolidatedContextForLlm`. Record
facts, not rules: "liposuction up to 1,500 cc (application.pdf p.4)", not "BBL sublimit applies".

## 1. Transaction type and forms

- **Transaction:** new business, renewal, midterm change (limits, specialty, hours, added
  insured), cancellation, ERP / tail (endorsement on a DPL policy), or Standalone ERP (run-off
  for a policy issued by another carrier). Say which, and on what evidence.
- **Application form:** DPLRRG application, renewal application, or a competitor's application
  (name the carrier). Note signed / unsigned, ink or electronic signature, and the signature date.
- **Application age:** the application signature date and the requested effective date, both as
  stated, and the gap in days when both dates are explicit (the manual requires an update when the
  application is more than 60 days older than the effective date).
- **Alterations:** any crossed-out or overwritten answers on the application, and any separate
  clarification sheet (who signed it, its date).
- **Other forms present:** DPLRRG Warranty Form, No Known Claims affidavit, No Known Loss / No
  Known Incident attestation, written request to bind, signed quote, Waiver of COI Request Form,
  supplemental applications (weight loss, MedSpa, entity, midlevel), ERP Request and
  Acknowledgment, Prior Policy Following-Form Acknowledgment.

## 2. Requested coverage

- Requested limits (per claim / aggregate) and any total aggregate stated; separate or shared
  limits for entities and allied providers.
- Prior carrier's limits. A request above the prior limits is a fact worth stating explicitly.
- Effective date, policy term, claims-made basis.
- **Retroactive date requested**, and whether prior acts are requested or the policy is
  **Retro Date Inception (RDI)** / "no prior acts" / "retro = inception".
- Deductible requested (amount or %), and any deductible already imposed by a prior carrier.
- Endorsements requested or currently carried (vicarious liability, DDR, nursing home drop-down,
  MedSpa, weight loss, BBL, ketamine, waiver of COI, ERP).
- **Credits or discounts requested** or shown on a prior / competitor rating (loss-free, part-time,
  group size, new to practice, risk management, deductible, schedule rating), and the expiring
  premium, so the Analysis can check each credit against the facts (Part 3).

## 3. Prior coverage history

- Each prior carrier with policy periods, claims-made vs **occurrence** form, retro dates and
  limits, as stated on declarations pages or the application.
- **Gaps or lapses** between periods, with the dates on each side (state the dates; the Analysis
  judges them).
- Prior carrier status if stated (e.g. "carrier in liquidation", impaired, insolvent).
- Non-renewal, cancellation or declination by a prior carrier, and the stated reason.
- Coverage that was not an individual policy: W-2 / employer-provided, agency locum tenens
  policies, Federal Tort Claims Act (VA, military, FQHC), residency program coverage.
- For ERP requests: the termination / non-renewal date, the date the ERP request was made,
  whether the prior carrier offered or declined ERP, and the last date of professional services
  per insured; the expiring policy's consent-to-settle / hammer terms (§8.1); for a departing
  physician, who owns the tail under the employment contract and any mutual releases (§8.1).

## 4. Claims and loss history

For each claim or incident: claimant / reference, date of occurrence, date reported, allegation,
status (open / closed), indemnity paid, indemnity reserve, expense paid / reserved, carrier, and
whether it arose on the **expiring policy**.

Also capture:

- The **valuation date** of each loss run and the carrier that issued it, and the years it covers
  (the manual asks for 10 years).
- NPDB reports, and what they show.
- Any patient complaint, demand letter, board matter, investigation, disciplinary action,
  licence restriction, DEA issue, or "circumstance that could give rise to a claim".
- For renewals: claims **first reported since the last renewal** (count and dates).
- The application's own answers to the claims and discipline questions, quoted, so the Analysis
  can compare them with the loss runs.

## 5. Provider profile

- Licence state(s) and status; licence type (full, **limited licence**, **NY Limited Permit**
  under Education Law §6524(3)); any affidavit of agreement or supervision statement on file; for
  a limited licence, the employer type (public agency, non-profit, 501(c)(3)) and, in Florida, years
  licensed in any U.S. jurisdiction and years since active practice (§5.10).
- Medical school; for a foreign / international graduate, whether FLEX, ECFMG or Fifth Pathway is
  stated.
- Board certification or eligibility (board and year).
- Provider type: physician, NP, PA, RN, CNM, medical student, intern, resident, allied provider.
- For students and residents: USMLE Steps 1, 2 CK, 2 CS, 3 as stated on the CV, the training
  program, and the coverage period requested (residents are limited to 60 days, §5.4).
- For a CNM: the delivery setting (hospital, birthing centre, home) and the delivery types
  (uncomplicated vaginal or otherwise) (§5.2).
- Residency / fellowship / military completion date, and when private practice starts (new to
  practice).
- **Hours per week** of practice (including rounds, records, consults), and any leave of absence
  or disability with its start date.
- Risk-management CME, especially **The Sullivan Group** courses with a passing score.

## 6. Specialty, time split and procedures

- Every specialty practised, with the **percentage of time** in each (the manual classifies a
  physician by any specialty above 25% of time, using the highest).
- The full **procedure list** with percentages or volumes where given. Quote these names exactly
  when they appear, since each one triggers a manual rule:
  - Obstetrics / deliveries; home or water births; birthing centres; elective abortion.
  - Neurosurgery, spine, orthopaedic, cardiothoracic, vascular, bariatric surgery.
  - Plastic / cosmetic surgery; **Brazilian Butt Lift / gluteal fat grafting**; **liposuction
    with the volume in cc** (above or below 1,000 cc of supernatant fat); Florida office surgery
    registration (Level II / III).
  - Interventional pain management; controlled-substance prescribing and written protocols.
  - Emergency medicine (and whether DPL would be the primary individual policy).
  - **Excluded-list names:** ozone therapy, UBI (ultraviolet blood irradiation), hyaluronic acid
    for penile girth, silicone injections.
  - **Sublimit-list names:** O-Shot, P-Shot, stem cells, hormone pellets (Biote / BHRT), dermal
    filler for penile girth, Lipodissolve / PCDC, any non-FDA-approved procedure, correctional
    medicine, chelation, ketamine therapy.
  - Weight-loss management and the drugs used (semaglutide: Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus, generics;
    other GLP-1s), and whether weight loss is supplemental to another full-time practice.
  - MedSpa / aesthetics: Botox and fillers, microneedling, PDO threads, injectables; **Botox
    parties** (how many, where, storage, alcohol); **PDGF / PDRN / "salmon sperm" / polynucleotide /
    regenerative** services and the **route** (topical vs injected); how long the provider has
    performed each aesthetic procedure (years of training / practice per procedure).
  - Telemedicine (states served), correctional medicine, addiction treatment.
- Patient volume, visit counts, reads per day (radiology), ER visits, urgent care encounters.

## 7. Practice structure, location and territory

- Every practice location with the share of time at each; office-based vs hospital-based;
  telemedicine breakdown.
- **County of each location** (it sets the rating territory, §2.4): stated, or looked up from
  the practice ZIP (source `ZIP lookup (Census / GeoNames)`); name every county a multi-county
  ZIP spans.
- Practice / entity name and form (PC, SC, PLLC, LLC, partnership); where it is domiciled; the
  services billed under the entity and whether the entity itself delivers care (§7.3);
  **ownership** (sole owner or co-owner, and the co-owners' names); whether each co-owner and practising provider has their own
  malpractice coverage (carrier, limits, retro date).
- Group roster: each provider with licence type, specialty, hours, location, carrier, retro
  date, limits; number of full- and part-time physicians (group size); FTE / slot basis.
- Employed RNs, MAs, LPNs and allied providers, and whether they share limits.
- Supervision: supervising physician for each NP / PA / CNM, collaborative agreements, and the
  supervising physician's coverage; midlevels the applicant supervises.
- **Third-party facilities** where the applicant works (hospitals, ASCs, telemedicine platforms,
  correctional facilities, medspas or clinics not owned by the applicant), whether the applicant
  is an independent contractor there, and any **COI** received (entity name, policy period, type
  of coverage, limits, additional-insured language).
- **Medical directorships:** the facility type (nursing home, LTC, ALF, telemedicine, pain
  clinic, addiction centre, medspa, other), administrative-only or with patient care, written
  duties or contract, home-based / virtual-only.
- **Nursing home / LTC / ALF exposure:** facility names, state, whether the facility carries its
  own in-force limits; for a NY ALF, its licence, whether it provides skilled nursing beyond that
  licence, and any regulatory citations, abuse / neglect claims or staffing issues.
- Owned labs, urgent care, surgi-centres or dialysis centres.
- **Locum tenens** coverage requested: days per engagement and per rolling 12 months, specialty.

## 8. ERP, DDR and cancellation requests

- Reason for termination: death, total disability, permanent retirement, leaving practice,
  moving carrier, cancellation (with the requested effective date and reason).
- Years continuously insured with DPLRRG (relevant to free ERP on retirement).
- Supporting proof present: death certificate, physician disability letter, retirement letter,
  entity dissolution proof, employer letter with last date of service.
- Whether a separate entity policy exists, whether the entity has separate limits, and whether
  the entity will keep operating, hold records or employ other providers.
- Premium payment status if stated (paid in full, in default, financed).
- Pennsylvania Mcare participation for PA providers.

## Where these facts go

- Provider-level facts (licence, board certification, hours, procedures, specialties,
  directorships): `applicantProfile.other`.
- Coverage, claims (including disciplinary and board actions), prior-carrier, entity, facility and
  ERP facts: `extractedFacts`, never repeated in `applicantProfile.other`.
- A fact that is required by the manual for this risk type but absent: `missingInformation`
  (see `references/missing-information.md`).
- Every fact in sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 that is present, and every conditional document that is
  missing, must be in `consolidatedContextForLlm`.
