# Follow-up Questions

Write questions an underwriter can paste into an email to the broker.

- One question per gap or inconsistency. Be specific: cite the document and the value, and the
  manual section when a manual rule is why the answer matters.
- `addressedTo` is usually "Broker". Use "Applicant" for clinical narrative and "Prior carrier" for loss-run validation.
- `question` is one sentence. `rationale` says in about 15 words why the answer matters to the decision.
- Order by importance: CRITICAL gaps first, then HIGH-severity inconsistencies, then manual
  conditions that need facts (procedure volumes, routes, facility COIs).
- Ask for no more than 10 questions. Combine related asks for the same document into one question.

The manual prescribes some questions; use them when the exposure is present:

- **Botox parties (§5.8):** how many parties are planned, where they take place, how the Botox is
  stored, and whether alcohol is served.
- **PDGF / PDRN (§5.8):** whether PDGF or PDRN (including "salmon sperm" or other regenerative
  products) is offered or marketed, and the route of administration (topical or injected).
- **Aesthetic procedures at renewal (§5.8):** how many of each procedure were performed in the
  past year, and when the provider was trained in each.
- **RDI loss-run waiver (§1.6):** a signed No Known Loss / No Known Incident attestation and
  confirmation of the fact pattern (new to practice, W-2 employed, locum only, federal service,
  re-entry).
- **Entity (§7.2, §7.3):** ownership split, each co-owner's carrier and limits, full provider roster.
- **Third-party facilities (§7.4):** a current COI from each facility, or the Waiver of COI
  Request Form with the facility list and attestation.
- **Medical director (§5.6):** written duties, whether any patient care or prescribing is
  involved, supervised providers, and whether the role is on-site.
- **Part-time (§2.11):** average weekly hours including rounds, records and consults, and consent
  to release facility schedules.
- **Prior coverage (§2.5):** declarations pages showing continuous claims-made coverage back to
  the requested retro date, and whether any claim is open on the expiring policy.

Example:

- question: "Application (p.3) says no prior claims, but loss runs show a 2021 paid claim of $175,000: please provide the claim narrative and explain."
- rationale: "Claims misstatement affects eligibility; grounds for rescission (DPL Manual §1.6)."
- addressedTo: "Broker"
