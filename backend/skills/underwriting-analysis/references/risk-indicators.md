# Risk Indicators

Evaluate and report as findings where supported. Cite the DPL manual section when a manual rule
is the reason a fact matters.

**Claims experience**

- 2+ paid claims in 5 years, or any paid claim > $500k: HIGH.
- Any open claim with a significant reserve: MEDIUM–HIGH. An open claim with an indemnity reserve
  over $10,000, or any paid indemnity in the credit's experience period, also rules out the
  loss-free credit (§3.1).
- **Open claim on the expiring policy:** the retro date cannot be rolled (§2.5). HIGH when prior
  acts are requested.
- **Renewal:** new claims since the last renewal trigger a surcharge of +50% (1), +100% (2), or
  +300% or decline (3+) (§4.5). 3+ new claims: HIGH.
- Frequency trend (claims clustering in recent years).
- Allegation patterns (repeated failure to diagnose, surgical error, same procedure).
- Prior claims on BBL / liposuction: heavy debit (§5.7).

**Licensure & conduct**

- Board actions, probation, license restrictions, DEA issues: HIGH. These also block the RDI
  loss-run waiver (§1.6).
- No valid licence in the practice state, or an IMG without FLEX / ECFMG / Fifth Pathway: HIGH
  (ineligible, §2.1).
- Limited licence or NY Limited Permit outside its conditions (unsupervised, outside the
  underserved area, outside the permit term): HIGH (§5.10).
- Deactivated NPI (registry record status not `A`): HIGH until explained.

**Coverage history**

- Gaps in coverage, non-renewal or cancellation by prior carrier: MEDIUM–HIGH. A gap or prior
  occurrence coverage means no retro coverage over that period (§2.5).
- Prior carrier impaired or insolvent: retro cannot be rolled (§2.5).
- Frequent carrier changes (3+ in 5 years): MEDIUM.
- ERP / Standalone ERP gating failures (late request, gap > 30 days, limits above expiring,
  unreported known claims, premium default): see `references/dpl-coverage-rules.md`.

**Practice profile**

- High-tier specialty or high-risk procedure mix (see `references/specialty-risk-analysis.md`).
- **Excluded procedure** performed (§6.1 list in `references/dpl-procedures-and-endorsements.md`): HIGH.
- **Sublimit procedure** performed (§6.2 list there, plus semaglutide §5.9): MEDIUM, raised by
  volume or related claims.
- Florida BBL or liposuction over 1,000 cc in an unregistered office: HIGH (§5.7).
- Invasive PDGF / PDRN, or undeclared regenerative services: HIGH (§5.8).
- Botox parties, aesthetic procedures newly learned (first or second year): MEDIUM (§5.8).
- Pain management (5–25% surcharge, §3.4); midlevel pain management or controlled-substance
  prescribing without written protocols (§5.5).
- High patient volume relative to staffing, solo practice doing high-risk procedures; radiology
  above 100 reads a day: HIGH (§3.5).
- New-to-practice (< 2 years) in a high-tier specialty: MEDIUM.
- Midlevel practising independently where the state requires supervision: HIGH (§5.5). CNM
  outside a hospital or without physician presence: HIGH (§5.2).

**Structure & vicarious exposure**

- Co-owned entity where a co-owner or practising provider is uninsured: MEDIUM–HIGH; cover the
  entity vicariously only (§7.2).
- Entity-only request with under 50% of providers insured by DPL, or an entity delivering care
  itself: MEDIUM–HIGH (§7.3).
- Work at third-party facilities with no COI, or a COI showing inadequate or expired cover:
  MEDIUM (§7.4).
- Medical directorship with patient care, controlled-substance supervision, or a home-based /
  virtual-only role: MEDIUM–HIGH (§5.6). High-risk directorships (pain, addiction, med spa) need
  special review.
- Nursing home exposure where the facility has no in-force limits: HIGH (§5.3). NY ALF with
  citations, abuse / neglect claims or poor staffing: HIGH (§5.3).
- Locum tenens beyond 30 continuous / 60 total days, or in a different specialty: MEDIUM (§2.12).
