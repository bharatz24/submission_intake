# DPL Manual: Specialty Programs and Restrictions (Part 5)

Source: DPLRRG Underwriting Manual v5.7.26. Apply the program that matches the applicant's
provider type or practice. Program conditions (endorsements, deductibles, limits) go in
`recommendedNextSteps`; program bars go to the recommendation as described in
`references/dpl-eligibility-and-appetite.md`.

## Pennsylvania (§5.1)

DPLRRG does **not write OB/GYN or pediatricians in Pennsylvania** (unlimited Mcare requirement).
For any PA provider, also note Mcare coordination for tails (§8.1, §8.2).

## Certified Nurse Midwives (§5.2)

- Covered only in a **hospital** setting under **direct physician supervision**; the supervising
  physician's proof of insurance is required. Preference for uncomplicated vaginal deliveries.
- Excluded: home births, water births, standalone birthing centres, elective abortions,
  deliveries without a physician present.
- Pricing: 25% of the OB rate; other factors apply; maximum credit 35% (25% in NJ and IL); debits
  as appropriate.

## Long-term care and assisted living (§5.3)

- The nursing home must have **in-force limits**; the **Nursing Home Drop-Down** provision applies
  to every policy with nursing home exposure.
- A nursing home or ALF may be listed only as a certificate holder or location, never as an
  additional insured or for vicarious liability.
- **Medical directors of nursing homes, ALFs and LTC facilities are excluded.**
- Pricing: up to 25% claims-free credit on the nursing home portion only; limits $250K / $750K
  ($100K / $300K in Florida); **$5,000 deductible** on nursing home exposure.
- **New York ALFs only:** may be written at $1M / $3M; $5,000 minimum deductible per claim, not
  waivable; LTC Endorsement attached; facility must be NY-licensed as an ALF and must not provide
  skilled nursing beyond that licence; **decline or refer** facilities with regulatory citations,
  abuse / neglect claims or substandard staffing. Does not apply to nursing homes, SNFs or ALFs
  outside NY.

## Medical students and residents (§5.4)

Students: third- or fourth-year, assisting physicians in clinical rotations. Interns: first-year
residents. Residents: post-internship specialty training (2–7 years), always supervised.
$1M / $3M; one master policy domiciled in Georgia; 100% earned after inception (flat cancel only
before inception); 60-day built-in tail; only in states DPL actively writes; **residents limited
to 60 days maximum**. CV review: students should have completed or be completing USMLE Step 1,
Step 2 CK and Step 2 CS; residents should have completed Steps 1, 2 CK and 2 CS and be preparing
for Step 3 and boards. Pricing per the pricing sheet.

## Midlevels: NP, PA, RN (§5.5)

- Eligibility: individually licensed, in good standing in every state served, within scope, in a
  specialty DPL writes, supervision agreement where required.
- Claims-made individual policies, $1M / $3M; shared limits with an entity or supervising
  physician case by case; tail or DDR if qualified (free tail on death, disability or retirement
  when criteria are met).
- Risk classes and ILF:

  | Risk     | Description                                                                    | ILF       |
  | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- |
  | Low      | Behavioural health, wellness, basic primary care, no procedures                | 0.25–0.50 |
  | Moderate | Family medicine, pediatrics, urgent care without high-risk procedures          | 0.50      |
  | High     | Pain management, aesthetics, emergency medicine, procedures beyond basic scope | 0.50–0.75 |

- Rating: base is the state's lowest physician class × ILF; 25% part-time credit at 20 h/wk or
  less; credits for clean history or employed / supervised status; debits for prior claims, board
  or licensing issues, or procedures without adequate documentation or supervision.
- **Excluded unless underwritten and endorsed:** independent practice without required physician
  collaboration; aesthetic procedures (microneedling, PDO threads, injectables) unless approved;
  pain management or controlled-substance prescribing without written protocols; correctional
  medicine, addiction treatment, or telemedicine in unapproved states.
- Documents: CV, supervisory / collaborative agreement, supervising physician or entity COI if
  limits are shared, application with full procedure list and percentage of services.

## Medical directors (§5.6)

- Covers **administrative** duties only (chart review, QA, oversight). Any direct patient care
  must be disclosed and underwritten separately. Duties must be defined in writing; proof of
  responsibilities may be required.
- Supervision of midlevels must be disclosed, especially where prescribing authority is granted
  or reviewed.
- Directorships of high-risk operations (pain clinics, addiction treatment, med spas) need special
  underwriting review: REFER.
- Not covered unless endorsed: procedures, prescribing, direct clinical responsibilities.
  **Home-based or virtual-only director roles are typically excluded.** Supervision involving
  controlled substances (weight-loss drugs, hormone therapy) may carry drop-down limits.
- Pricing: administrative-only typically **25–40%** of the Family Practice No Surgery rate; higher
  for high-risk services; shared limits standard, standalone case by case.
- Documents: written duties, contract if available, list of supervised providers / services.
- LTC / nursing home / ALF / telemedicine directorships are **excluded** (§6.1, §5.3).

## Plastic surgery: Florida HB 1561 (§5.7, effective 2024-05-11)

- Florida law: offices where liposuction removes **more than 1,000 cc** of supernatant fat
  (temporarily or permanently, so including BBL) must register with the Florida DOH; unregistered
  Level II / III office surgery can be fined **$5,000 per procedure**. Physicians performing
  gluteal fat grafting, and their offices, must carry at least **$250K per claim / $750K
  aggregate** or an irrevocable letter of credit (Fla. Stat. 458.320, 459.0085; no stacking).
- DPL rules: **$15,000 deductible** for BBL or liposuction over 1,000 cc; **heavy debit** if prior
  claims exist; the **BBL-specific endorsement** on every qualifying procedure.
- An unregistered Florida office doing these procedures is a HIGH-severity regulatory finding.

## MedSpa and aesthetic medicine (§5.8)

- **Endorsement package** (exposure-based: MedSpa entities and any physician or allied provider
  with aesthetic exposure): **MedSpa Endorsement #38**, **MedSpa Scope of Services Endorsement**,
  **PDGF and PDRN Topical / Non-Invasive Use Endorsement**.
- **Deductibles:** per the required-deductibles table in `references/dpl-procedures-and-endorsements.md`.
  Tiered deductibles apply when a procedure is added midterm; at renewal the procedure and its
  deductible must be specified, and ask how many were performed in the past year.
- **PDGF / PDRN:** covered only for **topical / non-invasive** use. Injection, infusion or
  implantation is not covered unless DPL approves in writing and endorses it; refer any such
  request to management and legal / coverage review. Confirm and document whether PDGF / PDRN is
  offered or marketed (including "salmon sperm" or other regenerative terms), the route, and a
  review of the website, social media and procedure list for undeclared regenerative services.
  The insured must notify DPL before adding PDGF / PDRN midterm; the exposure is not covered until
  the required endorsements are issued or revised.
- **Botox parties:** ask the §5.8 questions in `references/follow-up-questions.md`.
- Documents: consent forms and training certifications.

## Weight-loss practice (§5.9)

- Attach the **Weight Loss Sublimit Endorsement** to every weight-loss policy: semaglutide
  (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus, generics, anything with semaglutide as the primary ingredient) is
  covered at **$100K per claim / $300K aggregate**. Semaglutide liability is still emerging
  (gallbladder-disease suits; label warnings revised). Wegovy is approved for adults with BMI ≥ 30,
  or ≥ 27 with a weight-related condition; use outside that is off-label exposure.
- Class: Family Practice / General Medicine in each state. Usually supplemental to a full-time
  practice: exclude the regular practice if it is insured elsewhere.
- **Part-time discount of 40% only**; usually RDI, so no further discounts except case by case.
- Documents: informed patient consent form; weight-loss supplemental application if needed.

## Limited licence physicians (§5.10)

- All states: limited-licence holders may practise only for public agencies, non-profits or
  501(c)(3) entities in areas of critical need, and must be supervised.
- New York: three-year limited licence restricted to a medically underserved area, with a
  notarised Affidavit of Agreement with the NY DOH (amended if the location changes); extensions up
  to six years for those pursuing permanent residency.
- Florida: licensed in a U.S. jurisdiction for at least 10 years; if more than 3 years since active
  practice, 6 months' supervision by the county health director or a Board-approved physician
  unless the Board shortens it.
- Other states: check state requirements.
- **NY Limited Permit Physicians (from 2025-08-01):** may be written at **$1M / $3M** if all hold:
  active NY Limited Permit under Education Law §6524(3) for an approved training program or
  sponsored supervised practice; practice in a NY DOH-recognised underserved area with a notarised
  Affidavit of Agreement on file; supervision by a fully licensed NY physician in good standing,
  with a Certificate / Statement of Supervision in file; coverage term not beyond the permit's
  validity (three years; extensions need proof of renewal and continued supervision). Attach the
  **Limited Permit Physician Endorsement** (covers only duties within the permit, under required
  supervision, and authorised by the supervisor / regulator).
