# DPL Manual: Eligibility, Appetite, Referral and File Requirements

Source: DPLRRG Underwriting Manual v5.7.26 (effective 2026-05-07). DPLRRG writes **claims-made**
medical professional liability only. Cite sections as "DPL Manual §x.y".

## Eligibility (§2.1)

- A valid licence to practise medicine in the relevant state. No valid licence = not eligible.
- Board certification or eligibility is **preferred**, not required. Its absence is a rating
  and selection factor, not a bar.
- Foreign / international medical graduates must have passed FLEX or ECFMG, or be Fifth Pathway
  certified. Unknown status for an IMG is a follow-up question; confirmed failure is ineligible.
- Midlevels (NP, PA, RN): eligibility in `references/dpl-specialty-programs.md` (§5.5).

## Application and file rules (§1.2–§1.5)

- Every application is reviewed for accuracy and completeness.
- A competitor application can be used **to quote**; a DPLRRG application must be obtained at
  least once every 5 years. A signed, dated DPLRRG **Warranty Form** is required before binding.
- Applications must be complete, signed and dated (ink or valid e-signature).
- An application dated **more than 60 days** before the effective date must be updated, within 30
  days of the effective date, with a No Known Claims affidavit.
- Discrepancies between the application and supporting documents must be reconciled and
  documented in the file. Applications may not be altered; clarifications go on a separate,
  dated, sourced sheet initialled by the underwriter.
- All correspondence and documentation must be date-stamped; information obtained verbally must be
  documented with the date and underwriter's initials.
- A limit increase above the prior carrier's limits needs a No Known Claims affidavit and is
  granted **prospectively only**.
- **New business file:** signed DPLRRG application; **10 years** of detailed claims history from
  all prior insurers valued **within 90 days of the proposed effective date** (all incidents and
  claims, indemnity paid, reserves, expenses, occurrence and report dates, loss descriptions,
  closed-without-payment claims); rating worksheet with justification of deviations; immediate
  prior declarations page (period, retro date, limits, specialty, endorsements); written bind
  request; signed and dated quote (all pages); correspondence and notes; subjectivities.
- **Renewal file:** annual renewal application (full DPLRRG application every 5th year); updated
  report on all open claims, including prior carriers', valued within 90 days of renewal; rating
  worksheet if credits / debits change; signed quote if required; bind request. At renewal also
  check the claims database and attorney contact for each open claim and apply any newly
  applicable guidelines (§4.5).
- **Midterm changes:** requested in writing before the change date; limit increases or decreases
  need a No Known Claims affidavit; subjectivities fulfilled before issuance.

## Loss-run waiver for RDI / no-prior-acts policies (§1.6)

Loss runs may be waived only when **all** of these hold:

1. Retroactive date = DPL inception date (Retro Date Inception).
2. No prior acts coverage provided.
3. Signed DPLRRG application.
4. Signed No Known Loss / No Known Incident attestation.
5. No known claims, incidents, demands, patient complaints, board matters, investigations,
   disciplinary actions or circumstances.
6. The application discloses no adverse claims, licensing, disciplinary or underwriting concern.
7. The risk is not in the high-risk carve-out (below).
8. The underwriter judges loss runs unnecessary on the overall profile.

When all hold, an NPDB report is not required either (still allowed at discretion).

Typical fact patterns: new to practice (within 12–24 months of residency, fellowship or military
obligation, no prior individual policy); W-2 employed moving to private practice; locum-only
history under agency policies; federal service (FTCA, VA, military); re-entry after extended
leave with no practice during the leave.

**Carve-out (loss runs always required, even on RDI):** OB/GYN with deliveries, neurosurgery,
spine surgery, orthopaedic surgery, plastic / cosmetic surgery, interventional pain management,
cardiothoracic and vascular surgery, bariatric surgery, emergency medicine where DPL is the
primary individual policy, and any specialty performing BBL or liposuction over 1,000 cc. Where
loss runs are unavailable, an NPDB report plus a supporting attestation is accepted, before bind.

**Loss runs are required** when: prior acts are requested; a prior retro date is to be rolled;
any known claim, incident, demand, complaint, board matter, licensing issue, investigation or
discipline exists; tail / ERP is requested; there is a coverage lapse or gap; the account has a
group, entity, facility, medical director, vicarious or supervisory exposure; the risk is in the
carve-out; or the underwriter needs the history.

At renewal of a waiver policy, prior-carrier loss runs are not required unless new information
materially changes the risk. The waiver never waives the insured's duty to disclose; a
misrepresentation can lead to rescission.

**Waiver file must show:** RDI with no prior acts; retro date = inception; signed application;
signed NKL / NKI attestation; the underwriter's rationale and fact pattern; any NPDB report; and
confirmation the risk is outside the carve-out.

How to use this in the analysis: when loss runs are missing on an RDI request, say whether the
waiver conditions appear to be met, list any that are unmet or unknown, and leave the waiver
decision to the underwriter in `recommendedNextSteps`.
