# DPL Underwriting Manual: Quick Reference

Condensed from the DPLRRG Underwriting Manual v5.7.26 (effective 2026-05-07); cite answers as
"DPL Manual §x.y". The Analysis report applies the full rules. If a question needs detail not
summarised here, say so rather than guess.

## File requirements (§1.2–§1.6)

- Signed, dated application (DPLRRG preferred; competitor form can be used to quote, DPLRRG form
  needed every 5 years). Signed DPLRRG Warranty Form before bind. No alterations; clarifications
  on a separate signed, dated sheet.
- Application more than 60 days older than the effective date: update with a No Known Claims
  affidavit within 30 days of the effective date. Limit increase above prior carrier: NKC
  affidavit, prospective only.
- New business: 10 years of loss runs from all prior insurers valued within 90 days of the
  effective date; prior declarations page; rating worksheet; written bind request; signed quote.
- Renewal: annual renewal application (full application every 5th year); open-claim update
  valued within 90 days.
- Loss-run waiver (RDI / no prior acts) only if all hold: retro = inception, no prior acts, signed
  DPLRRG application, signed No Known Loss / Incident attestation, nothing adverse disclosed, not a
  carve-out specialty, underwriter agrees. Carve-out (loss runs always required): OB with
  deliveries, neurosurgery, spine, orthopaedic, plastic / cosmetic, interventional pain,
  cardiothoracic / vascular, bariatric, EM as primary policy, BBL or lipo over 1,000 cc.

## Eligibility and appetite

- Valid state licence; board certification preferred; IMGs need FLEX, ECFMG or Fifth Pathway (§2.1).
- Will not write: PA OB/GYN or pediatrics (§5.1); ozone, UBI, HA for penile girth, silicone
  injections; medical directorship of LTC, nursing homes, ALFs or telemedicine (§6.1); CNM home or
  water births, birthing centres, elective abortion, deliveries without a physician (§5.2); retro
  cover over uninsured or occurrence periods (§2.5); NY ALF with citations, abuse / neglect claims
  or poor staffing (decline or refer, §5.3); Standalone ERP with limits above expiring, a gap over
  30 days, unverifiable prior cover or known unreported claims (§8.2); ERP for non-payment, fraud
  or severe adverse losses (§8.1).
- Referral: invasive PDGF / PDRN (management + legal, §5.8); pain / addiction / med spa
  directorships (§5.6); entity-only cover for OB/GYN, surgical aesthetics, interventional pain
  (leadership, §7.3); Waiver of COI #51 (Director of Underwriting, §7.5); every Standalone ERP and
  late or high-severity ERPs (U/W VP, §8.1–§8.2); ERP with open or adverse matters (U/W Manager,
  §8.1); PA Mcare tail replaced by a Standalone ERP (Legal, §8.2); schedule rating beyond 25%
  (§3.4); loss-run waiver (underwriter and underwriting management, §1.6).

## Limits and coverage (§2.2–§2.15, Part 7)

- Standard $1M / $3M; Florida $250K / $750K; New York up to $1.3M / $3.9M; LTC $250K / $750K
  ($100K / $300K FL); NY ALF and NY Limited Permit $1M / $3M. Non-stacking; separate limits need
  the non-stacking language and a total aggregate of at most $5M.
- Territory: where more than 50% of time is spent. Class: highest specialty above 25% of time.
- Retro (prior acts) only with continuous claims-made coverage, retro limits not above current,
  solvent prior carrier; never over gaps or occurrence periods; not rolled with an open claim on
  the expiring policy.
- Part-time (20 h/wk or less): 25% credit for 10–20 h (15% major surgery), 40% under 10 h (25%).
- Locum: free up to 30 continuous / 60 total days in 12 months; 31–60 days at 35%.
- New to private practice (within 6 months of training): 50% year-1 discount.
- Suspended coverage: leave 45+ days at 25% of FT rate, max one year.
- Co-owned entity: vicarious only, Endorsement #26; COI if the entity has its own policy. Solo
  practitioners cannot have separate corporate limits.
- COIs needed for work at facilities the insured doesn't own, independent contracting,
  supervisory or director roles.
- DDR not extended to an entity with its own policy, separate limits or ongoing operations.

## Programs (Part 5)

- CNM: hospital only, direct physician supervision, supervising physician's insurance; 25% of the
  OB rate, max credit 35% (25% NJ, IL) (§5.2).
- LTC / nursing home: facility has in-force limits; listed as certificate holder or location only,
  never additional insured; NY ALF $1M / $3M with a $5K non-waivable deductible and LTC Endorsement
  (§5.3).
- Students / residents: $1M / $3M master policy (Georgia), fully earned, 60-day tail, residents
  60 days max (§5.4).
- Midlevels: licensed in every state served, within scope, supervision agreement where required;
  lowest physician class × ILF 0.25–0.75 by risk; 25% part-time credit; aesthetics, unprotocoled
  pain / controlled substances, correctional, addiction, unapproved-state telemedicine excluded
  unless endorsed (§5.5).
- Medical directors: administrative duties only, in writing; typically 25–40% of FP No Surgery;
  home-based or virtual-only roles typically excluded (§5.6).
- Limited licence: public agency / non-profit / 501(c)(3) in a critical-need area, supervised; NY
  Limited Permit at $1M / $3M with the Limited Permit Physician Endorsement (§5.10).

## Procedures, endorsements, deductibles (Part 6, Appendices B–C)

- $100K / $300K sublimit: O-Shot, P-Shot, stem cells, hormone pellets (Biote / BHRT), penile
  dermal filler, Lipodissolve, non-FDA procedures, correctional medicine ($10K deductible),
  chelation, ketamine (#49), BBL outside Florida (#46), semaglutide (Weight Loss Sublimit
  Endorsement).
- Florida BBL / lipo over 1,000 cc: $15K deductible, BBL endorsement, DOH office registration,
  statutory $250K / $750K minimum (§5.7).
- MedSpa / aesthetics: #38 + Scope of Services + PDGF / PDRN topical endorsement; $2,500
  deductible ($7,500 first year, $5,000 second year, $2,500 from year 3 for newly trained
  procedures); PDGF / PDRN topical only (§5.8).
- Weight loss: FP class, 40% part-time discount only, consent form (§5.9).
- Nursing home exposure: drop-down provision, $5K deductible, facility must have in-force limits
  (§5.3).

## Credits and surcharges (Parts 3–4, Appendix A)

- Loss-free (3+ years continuous claims-made, no paid indemnity, no reserve over $10K): 5% / 15% /
  25% for 3–5 / 6–10 / 11+ years. Sullivan Group CME: 5% at next renewal. Group size 5–31+: 5–20%.
  Schedule rating up to 25% (50% rarely). Deductible credits 2–10% (none on an imposed deductible).
  Combined credits: the manual says at most 75% off manual, with exceptions up to 50% (corporate /
  partnership); the wording is ambiguous, so flag it rather than resolve it (§4.1, Appendix A).
- Renewal surcharge for new claims: +50% (1), +100% (2), +300% or decline (3+). Pain management
  5–25%.
- ERP: 150% / 175% / 200% / 250% for 1–4 years, unlimited 500% (PA Mcare only); Unlimited ERP and
  Standalone ERP 270% (Standalone adds a surcharge); free on death, disability, or retirement after
  5 years with DPLRRG (§2.9, §8.1, §8.2).
